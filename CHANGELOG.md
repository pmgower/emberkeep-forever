## 0.4.0

- Professions tab: every alt's profession skills side by side, with a column for each profession, including Cooking, Fishing and First Aid
- See at a glance which professions none of your alts have (gray titles), and which alts share one
- Click a column title to sort by that skill; hover it for the full name
- Log in on each alt once to read its professions

## 0.3.1

- Rested tab: a Well Rested column shows each alt's Legacy rank, and the tooltip says what it does
- Fixed: rested XP and time until full now use each alt's own Well Rested rank, not the rank of the character you last played
- Rested percentages show one decimal on the Rested tab and in row tooltips
- Log in on each alt once to read its rank; until then it shows "Not read yet" and counts as 0

## 0.3.0

- Click an alt to open a panel beside the window with their gear: item names, item levels, tooltips, and shift-click to link
- Bags tab in the same panel: what each alt is carrying and how many slots are free
- Up and Down step through your alts, and the open tab stays open
- Empty slots and outleveled items are marked
- Gear shows in two columns like the Character window, or as a list
- Emberkeep button on the Character window
- Log in on each alt once to save their gear and bags

## 0.2.0

- Renamed from Kinfolk: Forever (if you installed by hand, delete the old Kinfolk folder, then log in on each alt once to fill your list again)
- Rested tab: Zone column next to Inn/Outside, and the tooltip names the inn or city district
- Overview tab: time played for each alt, plus a total
- Characters page in the options to remove alts you've deleted, or right-click an alt in the window
- Report a bug with /emberkeep feedback or ctrl-click on the minimap button, and Emberkeep offers a report if it hits an error
- Character and Rested columns widen as you resize the window
- Fixed the cut-off login reminder label in the options

## 0.1.0

- Rested tab: who to play next, with full alts first, a rested bar for each alt, and time until full
- Overview tab: every alt's level, gold, zone and last seen, plus your total gold
- Themed window: movable, resizable, with 4 color schemes and Esc to close
- Minimap button with a "Play next" tooltip, plus an optional login reminder
- Options page: color scheme, window scale, lock, first names only, and forget a character
