## 0.1.0

- Rested tab: who to play next, with full alts first, a rested bar for each alt, and time until full
- Overview tab: every alt's level, gold, zone and last seen, plus your total gold
- Themed window: movable, resizable, with 4 color schemes and Esc to close
- Minimap button with a "Play next" tooltip, plus an optional login reminder
- Options page: color scheme, window scale, lock, first names only, and forget a character
